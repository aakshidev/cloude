Link your medical history

What is Health sync? Health sync is an optional connection between your health portal and Cloude. When you turn it on and sign in to your portal, Cloude can read your medical history, such as existing conditions and past care, and use it to tailor the steps it suggests to your health instead of giving general advice. It is off by default, only works with your consent, and you can switch it off and delete your linked history any time.

Questions

Is my data private?

Your check-in answers stay in this page. If you choose to sync your medical history, it is saved to your Cloude profile and you can delete it any time.

Is this a medical test?

No. It's a self-reflection tool to help you notice patterns. It can't diagnose or treat anxiety.

What if I feel worse?

Pause, try the grounding exercise, and reach out to someone you trust or a health professional.

Do I have to link my medical history?

No. Everything else works without it.

Allow Digital detox?

Digital detox will manage your screen time on this device. It tracks the time on the apps you choose, sends you reminders and limit alerts, and applies your quiet hours. Your browser will also ask to show notifications.

You can turn it off at any time. Everything stays on this device.

Digital detox

Screen time limits, reminders and quiet hours.

Cloude runs in your browser, so it counts the time you log with Start and Stop on each app, and alerts arrive while Cloude is open in a tab.

1Screen time limits

Add the apps you want to limit and set a daily time. Press Start when you open an app and Stop when you leave. Cloude tells you how much time is left and when you go over.

2Reminders

Water, nutrition, collagen or anything else. Pick a preset or set your own time on the clock.

3Quiet hours

Cloude sends a notice when quiet hours start and end, hides announcements and shows a calm banner.

4Your progress

Bars are minutes on your apps each day, the dashed line is that day's limit and red bars went over. A summary is sent when quiet hours begin.
